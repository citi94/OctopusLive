Octo Live - Support

Octo Live shows your home's live electricity usage from an Octopus Energy Home Mini, in the app and in home screen and lock screen widgets.

Contact

Questions, problems or suggestions: email octolive.app@icloud.com, or open an issue on GitHub.

Getting started

  1. You need an Octopus Energy account and an Octopus Home Mini plugged in and online.
  2. Sign in at octopus.energy, open your account, then Personal details → API access to find your API key (starts with sk_live_). Your account number (starts with A-) is on your dashboard and bills.
  3. Enter both in Octo Live and tap Connect.

Common questions

"Connected, but no live data yet." The Home Mini isn't sending real-time readings. Check it's plugged in and connected to Wi-Fi; data can take a few minutes to start after it comes online.

The widget isn't as up to date as the app. iOS decides how often widgets refresh (usually every 15–30 minutes). Tap the refresh arrow on the widget, or open the app for updates every 40 seconds.

"No Home Mini found on this account." Octo Live needs a Home Mini; smart meter data alone isn't real-time. Octopus customers can request one free from Octopus.

Removing your data. Use Delete All Data in the app's Settings, or delete the app. See the privacy policy.
